Custom Counter Mats: How Checkout Counters Become Selling Space

Your counter already has an audience. Give it something worth looking at.

October 6, 2026

A customer walks up to your counter. They wait, pull out their card, and glance down.

What do they see?

With custom counter mats, that everyday moment becomes an opportunity to introduce a service, highlight an offer, or give someone a reason to come back.

You already have the counter. You already have the customer. Give that space something useful to say.

What is a custom counter mat?

A custom counter mat is a printed, branded mat that sits flat on a checkout counter, reception desk, or service counter.

Unlike a flyer that gets picked up or a brochure that needs replenishing, a counter mat keeps your message where customers pay, check in, and ask questions.

That makes it a simple addition to your point-of-purchase marketing—reaching people while they’re already interacting with your business.

What could your counter be selling?

Start with something customers might appreciate knowing.

  • Dental offices: Payment options, cosmetic services, or a patient referral program.
  • Auto dealerships and repair shops: Maintenance packages, tire promotions, or financing options.
  • Retail stores: Loyalty rewards, product care, or upcoming launches.
  • Cafés and restaurants: Catering, gift cards, or online ordering.
  • Salons and spas: Memberships, service packages, or take-home products.

The message doesn’t have to be a discount. Sometimes “We offer catering” is enough to start a conversation—and a future order.

Think about the questions your team answers regularly, or the services customers are surprised you offer. Those are good starting points for a counter mat.

Common counter mat sizes

Three common sizes for custom printed counter mats are 9 × 14, 10 × 15, and 11 × 17 inches.

SizeWhere it fits
9 × 14 inchesCompact reception desks and checkout areas where space is limited.
10 × 15 inchesA versatile middle ground with room for a headline, supporting graphic, and call to action.
11 × 17 inchesWider service counters where a larger message or visual can stand out.

Measure the open space customers actually see. Payment terminals, monitors, and paperwork still need somewhere to go.

Bigger isn’t automatically better. A smaller mat with a clear message can be more useful than a large one that ends up underneath the card reader.

A practical surface for everyday business

Counter mats share space with keys, shopping bags, paperwork, and the occasional coffee cup. Material matters as much as the artwork.

Hard-surface counter mats made with 6 mil white vinyl provide a smooth printed face for bold colors, product photography, and readable text. The white material gives the artwork a consistent base instead of letting the countertop’s color or pattern compete with it.

“6 mil” refers to the vinyl’s thickness—0.006 inch—not necessarily the thickness of the entire finished mat.

For routine cleaning, follow the finished product’s care instructions. A surface suited to wipe-downs isn’t automatically compatible with every disinfectant or cleaning chemical.

Design for a glance

A customer shouldn’t need to study your counter mat to understand it.

Give the design:

  • One clear headline. Lead with the offer or benefit.
  • Readable text. Make the main message large and keep supporting copy brief.
  • Strong contrast. Help the words stand out from the background.
  • A useful next step. “Ask about payment options” or “Scan to join our rewards program.”

A QR code can connect your printed message to a booking page, menu, or signup form. Tell people what they’ll get when they scan, and keep the code away from countertop clutter.

Your logo identifies the business. The message gives customers a reason to act.

One message across many locations

Branded counter mats are also useful for companies working through dealers, merchants, franchisees, or partner locations.

They give participating businesses a consistent way to introduce a brand or service, right where customer conversations happen.

For longer-running campaigns, keep the printed message evergreen. “Explore our payment options” has a longer useful life than an offer with a deadline next month. A QR code linking to a webpage you control can carry details that change.

Need visibility from farther away? Pair the mat with a matching counter sign or custom table tent. The upright display catches attention as customers approach; the mat puts the details in front of them.

Put your counter to work

Good marketing doesn’t always need more space. Sometimes it needs a better use of the space you already have.

A counter mat gives an overlooked surface a purpose: answering a question, introducing a service, or turning a routine checkout into the start of another sale.
