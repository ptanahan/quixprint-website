Privacy Policy

How Quixprint collects, uses, and protects information.

Last updated: July 21, 2026. This policy describes how Quixprint, LLC handles information submitted through or generated by this website.

Our commitment to privacy

Quixprint, LLC understands the importance of protecting the privacy of visitors to Quixprint.com. We use information collected through the website to operate and improve the site, respond to inquiries, provide quotes and services, and communicate with customers and prospective customers.

Information we may collect

We may collect information that you voluntarily provide when you contact us, request a quote, communicate by email, or otherwise interact with Quixprint. This may include your name, company name, email address, telephone number, project specifications, artwork, shipping or billing details, and other information relevant to your request or order.

Our website hosting and analytics providers may also automatically collect technical information such as your IP address, browser type, referring page, pages visited, device information, and the date and time of your visit.

How we use information

We may use collected information to:

  • Respond to questions and quote requests.
  • Produce, manage, bill for, and fulfill printing projects.
  • Communicate about current or potential projects.
  • Improve our website, services, marketing, and customer experience.
  • Maintain business records and comply with legal obligations.
  • Send marketing communications where permitted by law.

Sharing of information

Quixprint does not sell personal information. We may share information with service providers and vendors that assist with website hosting, analytics, payment processing, production, shipping, mailing, communications, or other business operations. These providers are permitted to use information only as necessary to provide services to Quixprint or as required by law.

We may also disclose information when required by law, legal process, or governmental request, or when reasonably necessary to protect Quixprint, our customers, or others.

Cookies and analytics

The website and its hosting or analytics providers may use cookies or similar technologies to operate the site, understand website traffic, and improve performance. You can control cookies through your browser settings, although disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality.

Links to other websites

Quixprint.com may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, security, or content of those websites. We encourage you to review their privacy policies when leaving our site.

Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information under our control. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your choices

You may opt out of promotional emails by using the unsubscribe instructions included in the communication or by contacting us. You may also contact us to ask about, correct, or request deletion of personal information, subject to applicable legal and business-record requirements.
