Custom Counter Mats: How Checkout Counters Become Selling Space

Your counter already has an audience. Give it something worth looking at.

October 6, 2026

A customer walks up to the counter, pulls out a card, and waits. Where are they looking? Often, straight down.

Custom counter mats put your message right there. They turn a checkout counter, reception desk, or service counter into a place to introduce an offer, explain a service, or start a conversation.

But a mat has to do more than look good on day one. It needs to survive spills, paperwork, and daily wipe-downs. Here’s how to spec one that earns its space.

Give the counter a job

The strongest counter mat starts with a useful message:

  • Dental offices: introduce payment options or services patients may not know about.
  • Auto dealerships and service departments: highlight maintenance plans, financing, or seasonal offers.
  • Retail stores: promote rewards, product registration, or an upcoming launch.
  • Cafés: point customers toward catering, gift cards, or loyalty signups.

Think of it as a conversation starter that’s already in place when the customer arrives.

Choose the size before the artwork

Common custom counter mat sizes include 11 × 17, 12 × 18, and 16 × 20 inches. Available dimensions depend on the construction and supplier.

Measure the usable space first. Card readers, monitors, brochure holders, and paperwork all need room. A mat that fits comfortably is more likely to stay on display.

For a rollout across multiple locations, send a paper outline or sample to a few representative counters before approving the size. Finding out it’s too big after shipping 500 of them is an expensive way to measure.

Specify the surface and the backing

“Rubber-backed” tells you about the underside. It doesn’t tell you how the printed surface handles cleaning. Ask about both.

  • Hard-surface mats: vinyl or other protected surfaces can provide a wipeable face. Confirm how the graphics are protected and which cleaners are compatible.
  • Fabric-top mats: offer a softer feel, but need a cleaning routine suited to the fabric. Check suitability before choosing them for spill-prone counters.
  • Rubber or foam backing: affects grip, cushioning, and thickness. Request a sample to check how the finished mat sits on the actual counter.

Check the edges, too. Ask how the layers are bonded, whether edge protection is available, and how the mat holds up to repeated wiping. If customers sign paperwork on it, test that as well.

“Wipeable” isn’t the whole cleaning spec

A damp cloth and a disinfectant wipe are different tests.

Tell your printer exactly what staff use to clean the counter and how often. Ask for written care instructions and confirmation that the surface, printing, and backing suit that routine.

For a larger order, test a production-equivalent sample with the approved cleaning method. Watch for fading, tackiness, curling, or separating edges.

The right material is the one that works in the location—not just in the proof.

Design for a glance

A counter mat isn’t a brochure. Keep the message easy to understand while someone is paying, checking in, or talking to an employee.

  1. Lead with one message. One offer, one service, or one reason to ask a question.
  2. Make the headline readable. Print the artwork at actual size and check it from the customer’s standing position.
  3. Use strong contrast. Test a sample under the location’s lighting; reflections can make glossy surfaces harder to read.
  4. Give the customer a next step. “Ask about payment options” or “Scan to join our rewards program” gives the message a purpose.

Using a QR code? Leave clear space around it, keep it away from equipment and edges, and test the finished-size print on multiple phones. A beautiful mat with an unreadable code is a very expensive rectangle.

One mat. Hundreds of locations.

Branded counter mats are especially useful for companies marketing through dealers, merchants, franchisees, and partner locations. They give each participating location a consistent message at the point of purchase.

Keep evergreen copy on the mat and use a QR destination you control for information that changes. Confirm packaging, shipping addresses, and placement instructions before the rollout.

Need visibility from farther away? Pair the mat with a coordinating counter sign or custom table tent. The upright piece gets attention; the mat supports the conversation.

Before you order

Confirm the size, surface, backing thickness, edge construction, cleaning instructions, quantity, and shipping plan. Use your printer’s artwork template for bleed and safe areas, then approve a sample when the project calls for it.
