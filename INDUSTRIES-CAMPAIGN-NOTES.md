## Links and measurement

Example future campaign URL:

https://quixprint.com/industries/real-estate/?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=paid_social&utm_campaign=real_estate_print&utm_content=listing_hero_v1

Use consistent lower-case names. For a Google search campaign use `utm_source=google&utm_medium=cpc`; for LinkedIn use `utm_source=linkedin&utm_medium=paid_social`. Follow the chosen platform's actual campaign setup when launch time comes.

The existing GA4 tag remains G-D2CW5LWNT1. Its landing-page measurement receives the campaign parameters. Two new descriptive events use the existing tag only on the production domain:

- `industry_product_click`: `industry`, `product_id`, `cta`.
- `industry_cta_click`: `industry`, `cta`, `product_id` (empty for general CTAs).

These are browsing/intent events, not confirmed leads. No additional advertising pixels, platform connections or paid services were installed. No conversion-import configuration was changed. Successful-quote conversion setup and platform-specific measurement should be reviewed when campaigns are built. The update does not add marketing-source fields to quote emails or change the quote payload.

## Copy boundaries

Keep the offer consistent with these pages: a quote based on the customer's actual products, quantities and specifications; one-time projects or recurring printing; no payment required to request pricing. Do not turn this into a guaranteed savings percentage, guaranteed delivery deadline, automatic reorder subscription or online payment offer.
